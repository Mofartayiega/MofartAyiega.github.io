I am currently a PhD candidate in linguistics in the [Department of Linguistics, Languages, and Cultures](https://linglang.msu.edu/) at Michigan State University. I am primarily interested in sociolinguistics and morphosyntax, with a focus on Bantu languages; particularly Swahili and Ekegusii. My research explores how semantic features such as animacy interact with grammatical structure and how language varies and changes across speech communities. I investigate variation between General Animate Concord (GAC) and grammatical noun-class agreement in Nairobi Swahili, examining the language-internal and social factors that shape agreement patterns across age groups. My ongoing projects include suffix ordering in Ekegusii, coordination and applicative constructions in Swahili, and the morphosyntax and semantics of reciprocity, including the relationship between reciprocal morphology and event symmetry.
I currently work with [Deo Ngonyani](https://linglang.msu.edu/people/deo-ngonyani/),  [Suzanne Wagner](https://wagnersu.msu.domains) and Alan Ke on projects involving variation, morphosyntax, and morphosemantics.
