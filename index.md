I am currently a PhD candidate in linguistics in the [Department of Linguistics, Languages, and Cultures](https://linglang.msu.edu/) at Michigan State University. I am primarily interested in sociolinguistics and morphosyntax, with a focus on Bantu languages; particularly Swahili and Ekegusii. My research investigates the interaction between semantic features (e.g., animacy) and grammatical structure, patterns of language variation and change across speech communities, suffix ordering in Ekegusii, coordination and applicative constructions in Swahili, and the morphosyntax and semantics of reciprocity, with particular attention to the relationship between reciprocal morphology and event symmetry.
I currently work with [Deo Ngonyani](https://linglang.msu.edu/people/deo-ngonyani/) and [Suzanne Wagner](https://wagnersu.msu.domains) on projects involving variation, morphosyntax, and morphosemantics.
